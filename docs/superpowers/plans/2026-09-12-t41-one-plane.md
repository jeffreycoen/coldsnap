# T41: one plane (0.5.40)

Every body draws on the flat plane again, and the net sags only faintly beneath it — a tenth of the depth it had. T40 set bodies in the bottoms of their bowls; that put the sky on a dozen levels, a moon at one depth and a planet beside a star at another. Now everything sits at one height and the fabric shows mass as a shallow dish rather than a hole.

- **The projection goes flat.** The screen projection is the plain one again — no sinking — for cubes, stars, ship, lines, gate ring, compass, plume, and hole alike. The per-body dip lookup from T40 retires.
- **The net sags a tenth.** Both parts of the well depth — the body's own and the stars' — scale by one tenth before the weave is bent. Measured under the map's bodies: dishes of 15 to 34 screen units at unit scale where they were 151 to 343, against a body radius of 19 — no dish deeper than the body sitting over it. The weave's brightening near wells is unchanged.

Drawing only: the physics, the generator, and every pinned number are untouched, and the acceptance proves it.

Choices made plainly in this plan: one tenth is the sag; the brightening stays as it is.

The whole change was applied to a fresh copy of the live tree at plan-writing time from this exact script: every anchor hit exactly once, the file parses and builds, no trace of the retired lookup remains, every acceptance line below reproduced.
