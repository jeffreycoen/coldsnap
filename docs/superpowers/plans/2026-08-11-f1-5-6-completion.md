## B — F1.6 Vision Is Line of Sight (full plan; supersedes the outline `2026-08-11-f1-6-line-of-sight-vision.md`)

**Design truth:** control (the influence field) keeps ownership — build rights, income, wash, tint. VISION — what the fog shows you — becomes line of sight: the union of what every friendly eye physically sees, traced over real terrain. You can see ground you don't own and own ground you can't see.
