# COLDSNAP: THE OUTBREAK — phase skeleton

The war's valley, both depots and both armies as they stand, with people put into the towns and hamlets and the dead loose among them. The sickness starts in the town nearest the valley's middle; six dead stand there when the war opens. The dead hunt any living body within 24 meters and bite once a second at arm's reach; gunfire within 40 meters and blasts within 80 meters draw them to the sound; the bell is silent to them; with nothing in reach and nothing heard, they stand. They walk at 6.4 meters a second, twice a man's pace, and take 116 points to kill. A bite takes 15 points and stamps the sickness; thirty seconds later the body turns, and its coat shifts toward the dead's grey-green as the time runs. A body bitten to death turns at once; a body shot dead is a corpse. Soldiers of either army turn too, and a turned soldier drops its gun. A medic kneeling on a bitten man clears the sickness. A bitten rider turns inside the hold and bites every rider in it; the hull drives on with the dead sealed in, and they come out when it unloads. One of the dead pressed against a wall course, a sandbag, a tower, a hull, or the mech while it has somewhere to go takes two points a second off it, armor or not. A heard sound becomes a destination: the dead walk to it and stand there. Buying a plan off the convoy's hand no longer arms the build bar; a hire goes on to placement. Two people live in every standing form with a door, three in a hangar or warehouse; yards, wells, graveyards, markers, field walls, ruins, and the depots hold none. People wear a brown coat and a bare head and carry nothing. They live inside their buildings, just inside the door; spooked by any of the dead within 24 meters or by gunfire within 40 meters they leave by the door and run at a man's pace away from it, and after thirty seconds with nothing in range they walk back to the door and in. The dead sense a person through the walls as they sense anyone and reach her by the door. They are hit by both armies' fire like anyone and trip mines and tripwires like anyone; a tower under CAREFUL holds its shot for a living person in its flight path. A person killed by an army's fire adds one kill to the other army's count and pays nobody; a person taken by the dead is a loss with no ledger. People are hidden in the fog as the enemy and the dead are, and are not eyes. The top bar counts the living people beside the dead. Inside a sealed hold a turned rider bites every rider at the turn and once a second after. A dead kill neither pays nor scores; neither the dead nor the people count on the market. The bell and the convoy's hand keep the war's buying rhythm; the loss is the war's depot breach.

The dead are a third side. Both armies' guns fire on them; they fire on nothing. They cannot buy, build, or shoot. They are not a commander. This asymmetry stands with no closing task. Everything else stays symmetric between the two commanders.

The module sits on the front menu beside the war, saves under its own key from the first task, and lives in its own files under `src/depot/`. The war's behavior does not change; the enemy army is untouched.
