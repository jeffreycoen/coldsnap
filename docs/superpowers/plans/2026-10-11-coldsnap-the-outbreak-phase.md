# COLDSNAP: THE OUTBREAK — phase skeleton

The war's valley, both depots and both armies as they stand, with people put into the towns and hamlets and the dead loose among them. The sickness starts in the town nearest the valley's middle; six dead stand there when the war opens. The dead hunt any living body within 24 meters and bite once a second at arm's reach; gunfire within 40 meters and blasts within 80 meters draw them to the sound; the bell is silent to them; with nothing in reach and nothing heard, they stand. They walk at 6.4 meters a second and take 116 points to kill. A bite takes 15 points and stamps the sickness; ten seconds later the body turns, and its coat shifts toward the dead's grey-green as the time runs. A body bitten to death turns at once; a body shot dead is a corpse. Soldiers of either army turn too, and a turned soldier drops its gun. A medic kneeling on a bitten man clears the sickness. A bitten rider turns inside the hold and bites every rider in it; the hull drives on with the dead sealed in, and they come out when it unloads. One of the dead pressed against a wall course, a sandbag, a hull, or the mech takes two points a second off it. Two people live in each standing house, three in each big form; they stand at their doors, run from any of the dead within 24 meters and from gunfire within 40 meters, and are hit by both armies' fire like anyone. The dead trip mines and tripwires like anyone. A dead kill neither pays nor scores; the dead do not count on the market. The bell and the convoy's hand keep the war's buying rhythm; the loss is the war's depot breach.

The dead are a third side. Both armies' guns fire on them; they fire on nothing. They cannot buy, build, or shoot. They are not a commander. This asymmetry stands with no closing task. Everything else stays symmetric between the two commanders.

The module sits on the front menu beside the war, saves under its own key from the first task, and lives in its own files under `src/depot/`. The war's behavior does not change; the enemy army is untouched.
